What you will learn

A flood breaks the only bridge to a small village. In this story, you will watch the people build a new one, piece by piece. You will learn to follow each step — what happens first, what happens next, and why.

Why it helps you

Every story hides small clues. Good readers learn to find them. This little skill will help you read any story better — in class, in books, and in life.
