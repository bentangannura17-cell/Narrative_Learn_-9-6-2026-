What you will do

This is the evaluative level, the last chapter of the journey. You will read how Singhasari got its new roofs, then judge the hard choices Pak Rosyad had to make — and say why you judge them that way.

Why it helps you

Fair is not the same as easy, and a good reason is worth more than a quick opinion. Weighing a decision and explaining it is a skill you will use far beyond this story.
