What you will do

You will read about a small village by the water. The village needs help — it needs clean water for its people. As you read, look closely. Find the special numbers and names hidden in the story, like little treasures.

Why it helps you

This is a skill for every day of your life. You read the news. You take a test. You follow instructions. Good readers find the right words fast. Today, you will practice — one clue at a time.
