What you will learn

Same three islands, same story — but this time you look at the language. Which words tell you when something happens, and who is speaking when the text does not say so directly.

Why it helps you

Time words hold a story together, and knowing who speaks tells you whose side of the story you are hearing. Both make you a faster, sharper reader.
