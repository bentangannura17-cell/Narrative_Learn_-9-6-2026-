What you will learn

Every story is built in three parts: a beginning that introduces, a middle where something goes wrong, and an end where it is settled. On Nusa Barat you will read about a mangrove fence, and name each part as you go.

Why it helps you

Once you can see the parts of a story, you can follow any story faster — and tell one better yourself.
